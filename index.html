Fixes & explanation

Choose which fixes to apply. Characters a fix would otherwise touch are left exactly as typed when its toggle is off - and removed instead of fixed once none of the fixes below can convert them.

Always kept, regardless of the toggles above: the Latin alphabet (a–z A–Z), digits (0–9), standard punctuation and ASCII math signs (+ - * / = < > % ^), Markdown syntax, and whitespace (spaces, tabs, line breaks).
